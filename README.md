# Quick Commerce Analysis – Zepto

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Zepto's quick-commerce business model, customer experience, technology, and competitive position.

## Analysis Covered
- Business Model Analysis
- Market Gap and Customer Needs
- Technology and Innovation
- Customer Reviews and Sentiment
- Competitor Analysis: Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket
- SWOT Analysis

## Tools
- Microsoft PowerPoint
- Business Analysis Techniques

## Project Deliverable
- Zepto Analysis Presentation (`ZEPTO PPT.pptx`)

## Objective
To understand Zepto's business operations, identify customer pain points, and explore opportunities for business improvement.
