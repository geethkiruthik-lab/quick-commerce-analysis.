# Zepto Business Analysis

## Project Overview
This project presents a business analysis of Zepto, a quick-commerce platform that delivers groceries and daily essentials. The presentation explores Zepto's business model, customer needs, technology, customer reviews, and competitive position.

## Topics Covered
- **Introduction to Zepto:** Company background and business mission
- **Business Model Analysis:** Revenue sources and customer preferences
- **Key Differentiators:** Delivery speed, dark stores, app experience, and customer convenience
- **Market Gap and Customer Needs:** Customer pain points and benefits of quick commerce
- **Technology and Innovation:** Technology used to support quick-commerce operations
- **Customer Review Analysis:** Positive, negative, and neutral customer feedback
- **Competitor Analysis:** Comparison of Zepto with Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket
- **SWOT Analysis:** Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats

## Project Format
PowerPoint presentation

## Objective
To understand Zepto's business model, identify customer pain points, examine its competitive position, and explore potential areas for business improvement.

## Deliverable
- Zepto Business Analysis Presentation (`ZEPTO PPT.pptx`)
